Process

How I build your revenue infrastructure

Instead of off-the-shelf packages, I use a three-phase process to make sure we're solving the right problems at the right depth.
1Where are we leaking?The Systems Diagnostic 2BuildSystems Implementation Sprint 3ScaleRevenue Systems Ownership
1
The "Where are we leaking?" phase

The Systems Diagnostic

Before building, we need a map. This is a 45-minute deep-dive into your current stack — where data breaks, where leads are falling through the cracks, and where manual work is slowing you down.

The Goal
Identify whether your infrastructure needs a quick fix or a structural rebuild.
The Deliverable
A written "Revenue Systems Roadmap" that clearly summarises 3–5 high-impact fixes, each with a projected ROI estimate.
2
The "Build" phase

Systems Implementation Sprint

You define the bottleneck — "leads aren't reaching the CRM," or "data is missing from our reports" — and I engineer the fix. I don't just "turn on" automations; I build, test, and document the entire pipeline so your team can own it.

The Goal
Deliver a functional, reliable core process that scales, handed over with complete documentation.
The Deliverable
A fully built, documented, and tested process integrated across your stack.
Every build includes a two-week support period where I monitor the system to make sure data flows, automations fire, and nothing breaks post-launch.
3
The "Scale" phase

Revenue Systems Ownership

I act as your fractional Systems Engineer, taking ownership of the stack with ongoing monitoring, troubleshooting, and continuous optimisation — a dedicated engineering partner without the full-time headcount cost.

The Goal
Keep your engine running at peak performance while you focus on the creative and managerial side of the business.
The Deliverable
Continuous maintenance, priority troubleshooting, and dedicated build hours for your future growth plans.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions founders and operators ask before we work together.
Do you write marketing copy or design campaign visuals?
No. Think of me as the engineer who builds the engine, while you (or your creative team) provide the fuel. I focus entirely on the infrastructure — the APIs, data pipelines, CRM triggers, and automations — that makes sure your content reaches the right person at the right time.
Are you a marketing expert?
No. I'm a Systems Engineer. I don't decide what you should say to your customers — I build the system that makes sure the message is delivered, tracked, and synchronised across your entire stack. I work really well alongside marketing agencies and creative teams who need a technical partner to bring their strategies to life.
What does your "<1m Speed-to-Lead" metric actually mean?
It measures system-initiated action. The moment a lead enters your system (e.g. via a form submission), my pipelines verify, categorise, and route that data to your CRM within 60 seconds. A human may not call them within 60 seconds — but your system will never be the bottleneck slowing down your sales cycle.
My current tools aren't listed on your site. Can you still help?
If it has an API, I can connect it. While I have core expertise in stacks like Klaviyo, Ontraport, and Segment, my work is grounded in data architecture principles — not just a specific brand. If you have a legacy system or a niche platform, book an audit and I'll let you know within 15 minutes whether I can support it.
Why don't you have "off-the-shelf" packages?
Revenue infrastructure is rarely one-size-fits-all. A business with 1,000 leads a month has different technical requirements than one with 10,000. I use an Audit phase to understand your specific "data leaks" so I can build something that addresses your actual problems, rather than relying on a generic list of tasks. After the Audit, I provide a clear breakdown of deliverables, timelines, and pricing for each subsequent phase — so you know exactly what to expect at each stage and how long the process will take.
